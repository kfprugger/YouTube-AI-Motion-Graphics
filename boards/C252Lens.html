Magnifier lens

For finding one value in a lot of data. A round lens glides over the table and magnifies only what's under it (the board stays put: no camera zoom); it settles on the answer, which is red, and a label pops.
Example data.

lens magnifyglidepop
tl.to(lens, {"--lx": "760px", "--ly": "286px", duration: 2.6, ease: "power3.inOut"}, t) // .lensin = copy of the table, scale 1.9, // clipped to circle(120px at var(--lx) var(--ly))
