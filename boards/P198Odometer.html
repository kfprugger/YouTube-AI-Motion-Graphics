Digits roll like a meter and land one after another, left to right. Use it when a number is the payoff.

Odometer

For a payoff number. Each digit is a tall strip in a clipped window that rolls through 0–9 twice and lands; digits land 0.25 s apart, left to right. Stronger than a counter for prices.
Example value.

tl.to(strip, {yPercent: -100 * (20 + d) / 21 ..., duration: 1.4, ease: "power3.out", stagger: .25}, t)
